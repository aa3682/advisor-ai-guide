# Tools

This section holds the calculators, checklists, and worksheets that turn The Adoption Path into something you can do at a desk. Each tool supports a specific step and explains what to do with the result.

## What belongs here

A checklist makes sure nothing is skipped when a step has many parts. A worksheet gives you a structure for gathering facts, so what you carry into the next step is complete and consistent. Each tool is meant to be filled out, printed, or copied into whatever document the firm already keeps its working files in — none of them depends on software beyond what most firms already have. None of these tools recommends a specific vendor or platform; where a figure a tool needs is set by law, regulation, or a platform, the tool sends you to This Year's Figures rather than printing a number that could go stale.

## What's here

- **[This year's figures](/tools/this-years-figures)** lists every regulated or platform-set figure the guide refers to — limits, rates, thresholds, deadlines, pricing, retention windows — with the year each applies to and its primary source, and is refreshed once a year. This is the only page on the site that prints those numbers; every other page names the concept and links here.
- **[Building Your Use-Case Inventory](/tools/building-your-use-case-inventory)** helps you list and rank the AI use cases worth pursuing, to use alongside Decide What AI Is For.
- **[Sorting Your Data](/tools/sorting-your-data)** walks through classifying what the firm holds, to use alongside Sort Your Data.
- **[Vetting a Vendor](/tools/vetting-a-vendor)** is a checklist for evaluating a specific AI tool or platform, to use alongside Vet and Choose Tools.
- **[Writing Your AI Policy](/tools/writing-your-ai-policy)** structures the decisions that go into a written AI policy, to use alongside Write the AI Policy.
